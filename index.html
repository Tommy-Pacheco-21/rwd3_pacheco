My Top Three Favorite Albums

These are the albums that mean the most to me, and keep me coming back to relisten again and again.

The Big Three

Discovery - Daft Punk

My favorite album of all time, absolutely no skips on this one. The French electronic duo has managed to perfect house music with fantastic beats and samples. These samples pull from 70s and 80s music, but are recontextualized in a new way that makes every one of these songs stand out on thier own. A definite must listen for any music enjoyer.

Favorite song: Face to Face

Imaginal Disk - Magdalena Bay

As the most recent entry on this list, "Imaginal Disk" is a shockingly dense, magical, and ethereal experience the moment I pressed play. The pop/rock structure of these songs, combined with neo-psycedelic effects, create a hypnotizing experience that has me gripped the whole way through. Mica's vocals feel like the musical equivalent of cotton candy, and pairs excellently with the dense production. This album will absolutely go down as a modern classic.

Favorite song: Cry For Me

Stratosphere - Duster

A melancholic slowcore masterpiece that has me reflecting on what life is truly about. The albums soft, lo-fi atmosphere accentuate the theme of being lost in space, all alone, with no one around. Occasionally, the album ramps up in intensity, creating a much more powerful burst in emotion than if the whole album had that energy throughout. I'm so glad that this album exists, and I'll forever cherish it from the furthest reaches of the galaxy

Favorite song: Inside Out
